Who we are

Wander is an independent travel publication founded on the belief that travel writing, done well, is a form of literature. Our editors and contributors are writers first — people who go to places not to check them off but to understand them, then come back and describe what they found with care and precision.

We are not affiliated with any hotel group, airline, or booking platform. Every recommendation we make is based on first-hand experience and editorial judgement. Nobody pays to be included in our guides.

Our values

01

Editorial independence

No paid placement. No affiliate pressure. Our recommendations exist because we believe in them, not because someone paid for the mention.

02

First-hand experience

Every guide is researched in person. We don't aggregate reviews or rewrite press releases — we go, we look, we talk to people, we eat the food.

03

Quality over quantity

We publish fewer guides than most travel sites. Each one takes time. We would rather publish twelve excellent guides a year than three hundred mediocre ones.

04

Responsible travel

We believe travel can be a force for good — when it benefits local communities, treads lightly on landscapes, and supports the businesses that make a place worth visiting.

Write for us

We work with a small group of contributing writers — people who combine deep expertise in a destination with the ability to write about it clearly and engagingly. If you are a writer with first-hand knowledge of a region we don't currently cover and believe you could contribute something exceptional, we'd be pleased to hear from you.

Please read our existing guides before reaching out so you understand the standard we are aiming for. Then contact us with a short introduction and one or two specific ideas.
