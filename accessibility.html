Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making Wander usable and welcoming for everyone.

Last updated: 8 July 2026  ·  Applies to: wander.example.com

Our commitment

Wander is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We believe that good travel writing should reach everyone, and accessibility is an essential part of delivering on that belief.

We actively work to increase the accessibility and usability of this website and in doing so adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines.

Conformance status

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities. Conformance with these guidelines helps make the web more user-friendly for everyone.

Wander is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working to address outstanding gaps and improve conformance.

Known limitations

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility, there are some known limitations. We are working to resolve these issues and aim to provide fixes in a timely manner:

  • Destination photography. Some decorative images within destination guides may lack descriptive alternative text. We are reviewing and updating alt text across our guide archive on a rolling basis.
  • Legacy embedded maps. Interactive maps on a small number of older guide pages may not be fully keyboard-navigable. We are evaluating accessible map alternatives.
  • Video content. Any video content embedded from third-party platforms may not include captions or audio descriptions. We endeavour to request and publish captions where available.
  • PDF documents. Downloadable guides provided in PDF format may not be fully accessible. If you require an accessible version of any downloadable content, please contact us and we will assist you.

If you discover an issue not listed above, please let us know using the contact information below.

Technical information

Wander relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1:

  • HTML5
  • CSS
  • JavaScript

These technologies are relied upon for conformance. We test with a range of current web browsers and assistive technologies, including screen readers and keyboard-only navigation.

Feedback and contact information

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the Wander website. If you experience barriers that prevent you from accessing any part of our site, or if you encounter accessibility issues not listed in our known limitations above, please let us know.

You can reach our accessibility team via our contact page. We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within five working days, and to propose a remedy within thirty days.

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility feedback, you may wish to contact the relevant national enforcement or standards body in your jurisdiction.

Assessment approach

Wander assesses the accessibility of this website through the following methods:

  • Self-evaluation using automated accessibility testing tools.
  • Manual keyboard and screen reader testing during development and before publication of new content.
  • Periodic review against WCAG 2.1 Level AA criteria.
  • User feedback — reports submitted by visitors are prioritised for investigation.
