Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wander ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Email address when you subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name and email address when you submit a contact form
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We also automatically collect certain information about your device when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and information about your use of the site through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our newsletter if you have subscribed
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Analyse and improve our website
  • Comply with legal obligations

Cookies

We use cookies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality, but the editorial content remains fully accessible.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating our website (such as email delivery services), provided they agree to keep your information confidential.

Data retention

We retain newsletter subscriber information for as long as you remain subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
