Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Wander, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, who we may share it with, and the choices available to you. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here. If you have any questions, please contact us at any time.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information we gather automatically as you browse the site.

Information you provide. When you subscribe to our newsletter or submit the contact form, we collect the name and email address you enter. We store only what you submit and never ask for payment details, passport numbers, or other sensitive personal data.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical data each time your browser loads a page. This includes your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, the pages you visit on our site, and the date and time of your visit. This data is collected through cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies (see the Cookies section below) and through the server logs maintained by our hosting provider.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect only for the purposes described below and do not use it for automated profiling or decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

  • Newsletter delivery. If you subscribe, we use your email address to send you our travel editorial updates. Each email includes an unsubscribe link so you can opt out at any time.
  • Responding to enquiries. When you use the contact form, we use your name and email address to reply to your message. We do not add contact-form submissions to our mailing list without your explicit consent.
  • Site improvement. Aggregated, anonymised analytics data helps us understand which articles and destinations are most useful to readers, so we can focus our editorial work accordingly.
  • Security and fraud prevention. Server log data may be used to detect and investigate unauthorised access attempts, bot traffic, or other abusive behaviour.
  • Legal compliance. We may process or retain data where required by applicable law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. These are required for the site to function — for example, to remember your cookie preferences. They cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.
  • Analytics cookies. We use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to count page views and understand reader behaviour in aggregate. These cookies do not track you across other websites or build individual profiles.
  • Preference cookies. These remember choices you have made — such as display preferences — so you do not have to reset them on each visit.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse new cookies, delete existing ones, or alert you before a cookie is set. Disabling cookies may affect some site functionality, but all editorial content remains freely accessible.

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the site. Each service receives only the data necessary for its specific function and is contractually required to handle that data in accordance with applicable privacy law.

  • Hosting (Vercel). Our site is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel processes server request logs — including IP addresses and page URLs — to serve pages and maintain uptime. See Vercel's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Email delivery. We use a third-party transactional email provider to deliver newsletter issues and contact-form replies. This provider receives subscriber email addresses solely for the purpose of sending messages on our behalf and is prohibited from using that data for its own marketing.
  • Analytics. We use a privacy-first analytics service that does not set cross-site tracking cookies, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not sell data. Aggregated metrics (page views, referral sources, country-level geography) are visible to us but no individual visitor profiles are created or shared.
  • Google Fonts. Our site loads fonts (Cormorant Garamond and Inter) from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address. Google states that it does not use this data to track or profile individual users; see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not use advertising networks, retargeting pixels, social media tracking widgets, or any third-party service whose primary purpose is to build advertising profiles.

Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Newsletter subscriber records are retained for as long as you remain subscribed; if you unsubscribe, your email address is deleted within 30 days. Contact-form submissions are retained for up to 12 months to allow us to follow up if needed, then deleted. Anonymised analytics data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be used to identify you.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights under data-protection law including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy in a portable format.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your personal data ("right to be forgotten") where we no longer have a lawful basis to retain it.
  • Restrict our processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent (for example, you may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority if you believe your rights have been infringed.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where changes are material, we will make a reasonable effort to notify newsletter subscribers by email. Continued use of the site after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please reach out via our contact page. We are committed to addressing your enquiry promptly and transparently.
