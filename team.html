The People Behind Wander

Writers, editors,
and wanderers

A small, independent team that believes good travel writing is worth doing slowly and doing well.

Editorial

Our core team

MK

Editor-in-Chief

Margaret Keane

Margaret has been writing about travel for twenty years. She joined Wander at its founding after a decade at a national broadsheet, where she edited the Saturday travel supplement. She has reported from forty-six countries and is rarely without a notebook.

TH

Deputy Editor

Thomas Hayward

Thomas oversees long-form features and the deep-dive guide series. He spent six years as a foreign correspondent in East Asia and holds an MSc in cultural geography. He is the author of two books on slow travel in Japan.

SL

Senior Editor, Europe

Sofia Larsen

Sofia specialises in Southern and Nordic Europe. Based between Lisbon and Stockholm, she has contributed to Wander since its second year and became a staff editor in 2021. She is particularly interested in the intersection of food, landscape, and local identity.

RO

Commissioning Editor

Ravi Oberoi

Ravi manages the contributor network and commissions new work for the site. He has a background in literary publishing and a deep knowledge of South Asian travel writing. He is based in London and travels extensively for research.

Contributors

Contributing writers

AN

Contributing Writer

Amara Nwosu

Amara writes on West and Central Africa. A journalist and documentary filmmaker based in Lagos, she brings a rigorous, on-the-ground perspective to every piece.

JM

Contributing Writer

James Morrow

James covers Patagonia, the Andes, and the wider Southern Cone. A former mountaineer and geography lecturer, he has lived in Chile for the past twelve years.

YT

Contributing Writer

Yuki Tanaka

Yuki focuses on Japan and the Korean peninsula. Her writing is known for its attention to seasonal rhythm and the smaller rituals of daily life that most travel guides ignore.

CB

Contributing Writer

Clara Bouchard

Clara writes about France, Italy, and the Mediterranean. A trained chef who turned to travel writing, she is especially attuned to the relationship between cuisine, culture, and place.

ES

Contributing Writer

Erik Strand

Erik covers the Nordic and Arctic regions. A native of Bergen, he is a regular contributor to several Scandinavian publications and has twice crossed Norway by ski.

PA

Contributing Writer

Priya Acharya

Priya writes on India and Sri Lanka, with a particular focus on the Deccan and the coasts of Kerala. She has a doctorate in postcolonial literature and teaches writing in Mumbai.
