Work at Wander

We're a small, independent team of writers, editors, and makers who believe travel journalism matters. Come help us do it well.

Wander is editorially independent, remote-friendly, and built around a deep respect for craft. We move thoughtfully, publish deliberately, and care about making things that last. If that sounds like somewhere you'd like to work, read on.

Why Wander

We're a small, senior team. Everyone here does meaningful work from day one, and there's no bureaucracy between a good idea and seeing it published. Here's what we offer:

✦

Editorial independence

No algorithm to chase, no SEO checklists. We write what deserves to be written and trust our readers to find it.

✦

Remote & flexible

Most roles are fully remote. We coordinate across time zones with asynchronous tools and one optional weekly call.

✦

Travel budget

All editorial staff receive an annual assignment travel allowance. We believe in going to the places we write about.

✦

Craft over speed

We publish slowly and deliberately. You'll have the time to make your work genuinely good rather than just finished.

✦

Competitive compensation

Salaries and day rates benchmarked to the top quartile of independent media. We don't ask people to work for passion alone.

✦

No open-plan noise

Focused, deep work is valued here. Meeting culture is minimal and considered. Most communication happens in writing.
