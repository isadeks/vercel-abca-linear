Visa Information

Entry requirements vary by nationality and the purpose and length of your stay. This page collects practical visa guidance to help you plan — always confirm the details with the official embassy or consulate before you travel.

Whether you need a visa depends on your nationality — not on where you currently live — as well as how long you plan to stay and what you intend to do. Below is a worked example for one of our most-requested destinations, Japan. For every other country, use the linked official resources to check the rules that apply to you.

Spotlight: Japan

The guidance below summarises the general visa information published by the Embassy of Japan in the UK. It applies to travellers whose place of residence is in the UK.

Who needs a visa?

The Visa Exemption Arrangement applies based on your nationality, not your UK residence status.

Traveller Purpose & length of stay Visa required?
British Citizens Short-term stay (less than 6 months) for tourism, business (without paid activities from the organiser/inviter in Japan), conference, or study purposes Not required
Nationals of Visa Exemption Countries and Regions Short-term stay (less than 90 days) for tourism, business (without paid activities from the organiser/inviter in Japan), conference, or study purposes Not required
Nationals of Non-Visa Exemption Countries and Regions Any purpose Required
Certificate of Eligibility (COE) holders Long-term stay Required

British Overseas Citizens, British Subjects, British Overseas Territories Citizens and holders of British Overseas Territories issued passports or Convention Travel Documents are not included in "British Citizens" or "Visa Exemption Countries and Regions" above, and must obtain a visa prior to travel.

How to apply

Applications from non-residents in the UK (e.g. visitor visa holders) cannot be accepted. There are three main routes:

  • At the embassy — submit all required documents for your visa category at the Consular Section in person. An appointment must be booked in advance.
  • Through a registered visa agent — available for most categories, except Student, Working Holiday, or Spouse or Child of a Japanese National visas.
  • Via the Japan eVISA system — submit your application, receive your eVISA, and pay the fee by credit/debit card online. eVISA is available for tourism.

Visa categories

  • Temporary Visitor Visa (eVISA available for tourism)
  • Work or Long-term Stay Visa
  • Working Holiday Visa
  • Volunteer Visa
  • Spouse or Child of Japanese National Visa
  • Medical Stay Visa

Processing time

Once an in-person application has been accepted as valid, it usually takes around 5 working days. eVISA (online) applications normally take about 2 weeks on average.

Fees & opening hours

Visa fees vary depending on the applicant's nationality and the type of visa. The Consular Section is open 09:30–16:30, Monday to Friday, excluding British national holidays, the year-end and New Year holidays, and some Japanese national holidays.

Jurisdiction

Visa applications should be made at the Japanese Embassy with jurisdiction over your place of residence. Applicants living in Scotland and the North of England (Cumbria, County Durham, Darlington, Gateshead, Hartlepool, Middlesbrough, Newcastle upon Tyne, North Tyneside, Northumberland, Redcar and Cleveland, South Tyneside, Stockton-on-Tees and Sunderland) should contact the Consulate-General of Japan in Edinburgh.

Other destinations

Requirements change frequently and differ for every country. Before booking, confirm the current rules with an official source such as:

  • The embassy or consulate of the country you are visiting
  • Your own government's foreign travel advice service
  • Any official electronic travel authorisation (eVisa / ETA) portal for your destination

Visa rules can change without notice. The information on this page is provided as general guidance only and does not constitute legal or immigration advice. Always verify entry requirements with the relevant official authority before you travel.

Questions?

If you have a question about a specific destination guide, feel free to contact us — but for binding visa decisions, please rely on the official embassy or consulate for your destination.
