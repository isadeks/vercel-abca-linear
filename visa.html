Visa Information

Practical entry guidance for our featured destinations

Visa rules change frequently and depend on your nationality, your reason for travel, and how long you plan to stay. Always confirm the current requirements with the official embassy or consulate before you book. Below is a general overview, with a worked example for Japan — one of our most popular destinations.

Before you travel

A few principles apply almost everywhere:

  • Visa exemption arrangements are usually based on your nationality, not your country of residence.
  • Requirements differ by purpose of travel — tourism, business, study, or long-term stay each have their own rules.
  • Your passport typically needs to be valid for the whole of your intended stay, and often for several months beyond it.
  • Where a visa is required, apply well in advance — processing can take from a few days to several weeks.

The information on this page is a general guide only and may be out of date. Wander accepts no liability for decisions made based on it. Always verify entry requirements with the relevant official embassy or government source before travelling.

Example: Visas for Japan

The following summarises the general visa information published by the Embassy of Japan in the UK. It is intended as an illustrative example — travellers of other nationalities, or those applying from outside the UK, should consult the embassy with jurisdiction over their place of residence.

Who needs a visa?

Whether you need a visa depends on your nationality. Japan operates a Visa Exemption Arrangement with a number of countries and regions.

Traveller Purpose & stay Visa
British citizens Short-term stay (less than 6 months) for tourism, business (without paid activities in Japan), conference or study Not required
Nationals of visa-exemption countries and regions Short-term stay (less than 90 days) for tourism, business (without paid activities in Japan), conference or study Not required
Nationals of non-exemption countries and regions Any purpose Required
Certificate of Eligibility (COE) holders Work, study or long-term stay Required

British Overseas Citizens, British Subjects, British Overseas Territories Citizens, holders of British Overseas Territories passports, and holders of Convention Travel Documents are not covered by the exemptions above and must obtain a visa before travel.

Visa categories

  • Temporary Visitor Visa (eVISA available for tourism)
  • Work or Long-term Stay Visa
  • Working Holiday Visa
  • Volunteer Visa
  • Spouse or Child of Japanese National Visa
  • Medical Stay Visa

How to apply

Applications are made either in person at the Consular Section of the embassy — by appointment booked in advance — or online through the Japan eVISA system, where you can submit your application, receive your eVISA and pay the fee by credit or debit card. Registered visa agents may be used for most categories, except Student, Working Holiday, and Spouse or Child of Japanese National visas. Applications from non-residents in the UK cannot be accepted.

Jurisdiction

Applications should be made at the Japanese embassy or consulate with jurisdiction over your place of residence. Applicants living in Scotland and the North of England should apply to the Consulate-General of Japan in Edinburgh.

Processing time and fees

Once an in-person application has been accepted as valid, it usually takes around 5 working days. Online (eVISA) applications typically take about 2 weeks on average. Fees vary depending on your nationality and the type of visa.

Official contacts

For general visa enquiries, the Japan Visa Information Hotline is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week on 0844-986-4968 (calls answered in English), or by email at japan-visa@bridge-ms.com. Full, up-to-date details are published by the Embassy of Japan in the UK.
