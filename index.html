From the journal

48 hours in Lisbon

"There is something about Lisbon's golden light at dusk — the way it catches the azulejos — that makes you feel like time has slowed to a gentle, aching halt."

Start your morning in Alfama, where neighbours still hang laundry between centuries-old facades and fado drifts from open windows. Ride Tram 28 uphill to the castle. By afternoon, descend to Bairro Alto for custard tarts still warm from the oven at Pastéis de Belém. End your day watching the sun melt into the Tagus from a miradouro.

Lisbon rewards those who wander without a map. Every steep lane is a discovery; every tiled doorway, a small work of art.

MF
Maria Fonseca Local contributor · Lisbon, Portugal

Smart travel

Essential tips

Practical wisdom from seasoned travellers to make every journey smoother.

🗺️

Go offline-first

Download Maps.me or Google Maps offline regions before you land. Connectivity is never guaranteed when you need it most.

🌤️

Travel shoulder-season

May and September offer summer weather at half the crowds and prices. The locals are friendlier when they're not exhausted.

💳

Carry two cards

A Charles Schwab debit (no ATM fees worldwide) plus one backup credit card. Never rely on a single payment method abroad.

🎒

Pack a day bag

A small 15–20 L daypack lets you leave your main luggage at the hotel and explore unburdened, even on day trips.

🍽️

Eat where locals eat

Walk three blocks from any major landmark and prices halve. Follow the lunch crowd — they know exactly where the best food is.

🔌

Universal adapter

One compact universal adapter beats carrying multiple country-specific plugs. Look for one with USB-C pass-through charging.
