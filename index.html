Essential Travel Tips

Make every journey smoother with these tried-and-tested tricks.

🎒

Pack Light

A well-organised carry-on beats a heavy checked bag every time. Use packing cubes and stick to a capsule wardrobe.

💳

Travel Cards

Use a no-foreign-transaction-fee card to avoid hidden charges. Notify your bank before departure.

🗺

Offline Maps

Download maps in advance. Cell data can be spotty — a saved offline map is your best friend abroad.

🌐

Learn the Basics

A few words in the local language — hello, please, thank you — open doors and warm hearts everywhere.
