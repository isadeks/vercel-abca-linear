Everything you need to know about how Wander works — from how we choose destinations to how we protect your privacy.

About the guides

  • Every destination we cover has been visited in person by one of our writers. We look for places that reward slow, attentive travel — locations with genuine character, strong local culture, and enough depth to justify a long-form guide rather than a listicle.

    We deliberately avoid destinations that are already over-documented. If a place has ten thousand near-identical articles, we'd rather put our energy somewhere that deserves more attention.

  • No. We do not accept payment for editorial coverage. No hotel, restaurant, tour operator, or tourism board pays to appear in our guides. Every recommendation exists because a Wander writer visited, evaluated, and genuinely recommends it.

    We believe this is the only honest way to do travel journalism. It means we publish fewer guides than ad-supported publications, but every one we do publish is trustworthy.

  • We aim to revisit each guide at least once a year. Practical details — transport links, prices, visa requirements, opening hours — change frequently, and we flag when a section was last verified at the bottom of each guide.

    If you spot something out of date, please let us know. We take accuracy seriously and act quickly on reader reports.

  • Short quotes with a clear attribution and a link back to the original guide are generally fine. Reproducing substantial portions of our text — even with attribution — is not permitted without written permission.

    For licensing enquiries, please contact us and we will respond within a few working days.

Planning your trip

  • Our destination finder quiz is the best place to start. Answer eight short questions about your travel style — pace, climate, budget, interests — and it will match you to one of our covered destinations. It takes around 90 seconds and requires no account.

    Alternatively, browse all destinations and filter by region or style.

  • Each guide includes a "Practical info" section with general visa and entry notes as of the last update. However, entry requirements change frequently and vary by nationality, so we strongly recommend verifying with your country's official foreign travel advisory and the destination country's embassy before booking.

    We flag the date each practical section was last reviewed so you know how current the information is.

  • Not currently. Wander is an editorial publication — we write and publish guides, but we are not a travel agency and do not offer bespoke planning services. Our guides are designed to be detailed enough that you can plan your own trip from them with confidence.

    If you have specific questions about a destination we cover, the best route is via our contact page — we read every message and help where we can, though response times vary.

Newsletter & privacy

  • Every newsletter we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom — one click removes you instantly, no confirmation required. We process all unsubscribe requests within 24 hours.

    If you have trouble unsubscribing, contact us directly and we will remove you manually.

  • If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and nothing else. We use it only to send you the newsletter. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

    Our site uses minimal, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which guides people find useful. No personally identifiable data is collected during regular browsing. You can read the full details in our privacy policy.
