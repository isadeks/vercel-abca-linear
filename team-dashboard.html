Welcome to the Team Dashboard

This view gives managers and stakeholders a live snapshot of the editorial team's workload and recent activity. All data is filtered by the date range you select above — use the quick-select presets (7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 90 days) or enter a custom range with the date fields. The dashboard updates instantly; no page reload is required.

Open items Work items created within the selected range that are not yet complete.
Completions Items that were finished within the selected range.
Avg cycle time Mean time from creation to completion for items closed in the range.
Recent activity A chronological feed of team actions — creation, comments, reviews, and status changes.
Team workload Open items per team member, with a relative capacity bar. Red bars indicate a high relative load.
